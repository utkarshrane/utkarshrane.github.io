About

The daily work is data and evaluation: annotation, prompt engineering, statistical and exploratory analysis, and both structured and unstructured data. I use Python, SQL, MySQL, Oracle, Excel, Power BI, Java, Git, Docker, and ETL workflows.

I also build precise searches and check that a source deserves to be trusted.

Live games

Four games on one host. A room code, no accounts, no install. Phones and laptops stay in the same place.

MERCURY

2–6 players

You share one bowl of liquid silver. Drag your hand through it. While you hold it, it sticks to you. When the clock ends, the most silver wins. The hard part is one liquid that every screen pulls at once.

CALL IT

2–6 players

Bluff dice. You only see your own dice. Bid how many of a face are on the table, or call the last bid a lie. A wrong bid costs the bidder a die. A true bid costs the caller. Last player with dice wins.

BLOOM

2–8 players

A shared floor of paint. Move, and your color stays behind you. Paint over someone, and that part becomes yours. Most color at the clock wins. A lead vanishes the moment someone cuts across it.

FATHOM

2–6 players

One sunken cave, three gold bells. Drag to swim, carry a bell to the moon pool, and touch whoever holds one to take it. Breathe on the bubble vents. First to surface two bells wins.

Next for the table: sound, a host that stays up, room for more hands, and a one-tap rematch.
